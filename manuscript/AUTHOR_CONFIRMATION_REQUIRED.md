# Author confirmation required before journal submission

The experimental and anonymous-review manuscript is complete. The following
items cannot be inferred from the public data or repository and must be
supplied or approved by the authors before an EMSE submission package is
created:

1. Author names, affiliations, correspondence address/email, and ORCID IDs.
2. Author-contribution statement (CRediT roles).
3. Funding statement, including grant numbers, or an explicit no-funding
   statement.
4. Competing-interests statement.
5. Confirmation of originality and that the manuscript is not under review
   elsewhere.
6. Approval to create a public immutable tagged release and a persistent
   archive/DOI for the replication package.
7. Final approval of the generative-AI disclosure in Section 8.3.

Until these items are supplied, the title page intentionally remains
anonymous and the Data/Code Availability statements identify the missing
persistent public archive as a pre-submission requirement.
